About

I come to production as a writer first. Every film I direct or produce starts with the question of what the story needs, and then I work out the schedule and budget that can deliver it.

That means I’m comfortable in the writers’ room, on a noisy set and in a budget meeting. I lead creative teams across fiction, non-fiction and branded work, and I’m currently building new originals at TVF.

Trained at SRFTI (PG Diploma, Direction and Screenplay Writing). Master’s and Bachelor’s in English Literature, Jadavpur University.
